STARD 2015 Flow Diagram
Prototypical STARD diagram to report flow of participants through the study

Type the number of participants (n) in each box. In the “Excluded”, “No index test” and “No reference standard” boxes, use the white text box to add your reasons, with the number of participants for each reason (one reason per line). Then save your copy as a PDF or Word (.docx) file. “Save as PDF” opens your browser’s print dialog: choose Save as PDF as the destination. Your entries stay in this browser until you clear them.
