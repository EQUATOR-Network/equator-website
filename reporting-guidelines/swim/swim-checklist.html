Synthesis Without Meta-analysis (SWiM) reporting items

Type the page number(s) where each item is reported in the “Page in manuscript” column. If the information is not in the review itself, use the “Other*” column to say where it can be found. Then save your copy as a PDF or Word (.docx) file. “Save as PDF” opens your browser’s print dialog: choose Save as PDF as the destination. Your entries stay in this browser until you clear them.

The citation for the Synthesis Without Meta-analysis explanation and elaboration article is: Campbell M, McKenzie JE, Sowden A, Katikireddi SV, Brennan SE, Ellis S, Hartmann-Boyce J, Ryan R, Shepperd S, Thomas J, Welch V, Thomson H. Synthesis without meta-analysis (SWiM) in systematic reviews: reporting guideline. BMJ 2020;368:l6890. doi:10.1136/bmj.l6890

SWiM is intended to complement and be used as an extension to PRISMA
